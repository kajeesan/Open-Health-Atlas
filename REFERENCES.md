# Research and method references

OpenHealthAtlas uses published work to inform selected equations, comparison
values, test descriptions, and educational prompts. The articles, figures,
tables, datasets, and PDFs themselves are **not** included in this repository.
The project records short factual method summaries and links back to the
original sources.

A citation does not make OpenHealthAtlas clinically validated, and a population
average is not automatically an individual target. Runtime wording must retain
the measurement protocol, population, uncertainty, and non-diagnostic limits.

## Energy estimation

- Mifflin MD, St Jeor ST, Hill LA, Scott BJ, Daugherty SA, Koh YO. “A new
  predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals.”
  *American Journal of Clinical Nutrition* 51(2), 241–247 (1990).
  [doi:10.1093/ajcn/51.2.241](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/51.2.241).
  Used for an optional resting-energy estimate; it is not a direct metabolic
  measurement.

The public system compares results only when the recorded protocol is
compatible. Gym-exercise loads are not represented as interchangeable with
isokinetic dynamometry, EMG, or direct clinical testing.

## Mobility context

- Harvey D. “Assessment of the flexibility of elite athletes using the
  modified Thomas test.” *British Journal of Sports Medicine* 32(1), 68–70
  (1998). [doi:10.1136/bjsm.32.1.68](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsm.32.1.68).
- Vigotsky AD et al. “The modified Thomas test is not a valid measure of hip
  extension unless pelvic tilt is controlled.” *PeerJ* 4, e2325 (2016).
  [doi:10.7717/peerj.2325](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.2325).
- Searle A, Spink MJ, Chuter VH. “Validation of a weight bearing ankle
  equinus value in older adults with diabetes.” *Journal of Foot and Ankle
  Research* 11, 62 (2018).
  [doi:10.1186/s13047-018-0306-x](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13047-018-0306-x).

Installation-configured thresholds and thresholds deliberately chosen below a
published normal value are labelled as project choices, not attributed to the
paper as clinical cut-offs.
