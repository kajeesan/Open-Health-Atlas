When you need a little more help

My AI won’t connect
  • No tools appear? Confirm your AI app supports local MCP, add the settings, then reconnect it.
  • The app moved? Copy fresh instructions from its new location.
  • No records appear? Check the workspace and dates. A new, empty workspace has no health history.
  • An analysis is taking time? Ask your AI to check the existing task. Some calculations take several minutes.

For your setup agent: the required connection type is local stdio MCP. The tools are health_catalog, health_query, health_analyze, health_evidence and health_task_status.

Switch records, update the app or disconnect

To switch records: open the workspace you want, copy its setup instructions again and ask your agent to update the connection. Switching the dashboard alone does not switch your AI’s records.

Before an update: back up your records, disconnect your AI and quit Open Health Atlas. After updating, open your workspace successfully, then reconnect the AI.

To stop access: disconnect or remove Open Health Atlas in your AI app. Closing the dashboard alone does not stop the connection.

Copy fresh instructions if you change the workspace, timezone or app location.

My records and backups

Use an empty personal workspace to start your own records. Importing an existing Open Health Atlas database creates a copy; it does not change the original or keep the two in sync. Other databases, PDFs and spreadsheets need a supported import tool.

Your records stay on this Mac when you update or remove the app. Find them in your user Library under Application Support → Open Health Atlas.

For a full backup, quit the app and disconnect AI clients. Copy that whole folder to a private backup location. Keep the original when testing a restore.

The app checks recovery copies before a database upgrade. Keep those copies if an update fails. Importing a database from another installation does not include its separate notes, attachments or connection settings.

The app is stuck, or I need support

First launch and upgrades can take a little longer. If the app stays stuck, quit and reopen it. Your saved records and workspace selection remain.

If an import is rejected, choose a compatible Open Health Atlas database or a healthy backup. If an update repeatedly fails, reopen the previous app version and keep your data folder intact.

For support, share your app version and what you were trying to do. Review the app information below first. Never share health records, database files, connection settings or provider keys in a public report.
